Privacy notice

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated

We handle your personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Health-related details are sensitive, so we collect only what is needed.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email (partners) and delivery address.
  • Orders and visits: items ordered, prescriptions you upload, hospital registrations, visit type and times, and payment status.
  • Partner details: business name, licences, contact and bank details for payouts.
  • Device information: basic technical data such as browser type, to keep the service working.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account, process orders and visits, and send sign-in codes and updates.
  • To let pharmacists check prescriptions and hospitals prepare your visit.
  • To take payments, prevent fraud and keep Hosirx safe.
  • To meet legal duties and improve the service.

3. Who we share it with

  • The pharmacy or hospital you choose, so they can serve you.
  • Delivery riders, who see only what they need to deliver.
  • Payment and messaging providers that process payments and send codes.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Prescriptions and health information

Prescription images and visit details are used only to fill your order or prepare your visit. Only the partner you chose can see them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any period the law requires for orders, payments and medical-related records. We then delete or anonymise it.

6. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No online service is completely risk-free, so please keep your sign-in code private.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, to object to or limit how we use it, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Cookies and browser storage

Hosirx uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in and remember your cart. We do not use it for advertising.

9. Children

Hosirx accounts are for adults. A parent or guardian may share a child's details when ordering or booking for them.

10. Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date above and tell you about important changes.
