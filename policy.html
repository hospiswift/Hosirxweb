Orders and refunds

How cancellations, refunds and delivery work.

Last updated

1. Medicine orders

  • Prescription items: need a valid prescription. The pharmacist may contact you if anything is unclear.
  • Delivery: the pharmacy sets the delivery time and fee, shown before you pay. A rider brings your order and you can follow its progress in the app.
  • Receiving: please check your items when they arrive.

2. Cancelling a medicine order

You can cancel before the pharmacy starts preparing your order and you will be refunded in full. Once preparation or delivery has started, please contact the pharmacy through Hosirx. A cancellation may not be possible.

3. Refunds for medicine

For safety, medicines cannot normally be returned once they have left the pharmacy. You are entitled to a refund or replacement if an item is:

  • wrong, missing or damaged,
  • expired or past its safe-use date, or
  • not supplied because the pharmacist could not fill it.

Report the problem as soon as you can, ideally within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo if possible.

4. Hospital visits and registration

  • Rescheduling or cancelling: contact the hospital as early as you can. Each hospital sets its own rules, and these are shown where the fee is shown.
  • If the hospital cancels or cannot see you, you will be offered a new time or a full refund.
  • Missed visits: if you do not attend, the hospital may keep the fee as set out in its rules.
  • Registration fees are for opening a patient record and may not be refundable once the record is created.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. This usually takes 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank or card provider.

6. Emergencies

For an emergency, call 112 or go straight to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for an online booking or delivery.

7. If something goes wrong

Email us with your order or visit number and what happened. We will review it with the pharmacy or hospital and reply as soon as we can. If you are not happy with the result, you may raise the matter with the relevant regulator.
