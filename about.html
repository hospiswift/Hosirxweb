What Hosirx does

Hosirx brings pharmacies and hospitals together in one place. Customers can order medicine from a pharmacy near them, and register and pay for hospital visits online. Pharmacies and hospitals use Hosirx to list their business, set their own fees and manage orders and visits.

For customers

Compare nearby pharmacies, upload a prescription, pay online and follow your order. Register at a hospital and get a ticket for reception.

For partners

List your pharmacy or hospital, set your own prices and visit fees, and manage everything from one dashboard.

How it works

  • Choose a pharmacy or hospital from the list.
  • Add medicine to your cart, or choose a hospital visit.
  • Pay online. The fee you see is the fee you pay.
  • Receive your medicine, or show your ticket at the hospital.

What we stand for

  • Clear prices. Partners set their own fees and you see them before you pay.
  • Safe medicine. Prescription-only items need a valid prescription and are checked by a pharmacist.
  • Your data stays yours. Read our privacy notice.
  • Emergencies come first. If someone is in danger, call 112. Do not wait for an online booking.
Hosirx is a platform that connects you with independent pharmacies and hospitals. We do not give medical advice. Pharmacies and hospitals are responsible for the care and products they provide.
