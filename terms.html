1. Agreeing to these terms

By creating an account or using Hosirx you agree to these terms and to our privacy notice and orders and refunds policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Hosirx.

2. Who can use Hosirx

You must be at least 18 to create an account. A parent or guardian may order medicine or register a visit for a child in their care. You must give accurate details, including your phone number and delivery address, and keep your sign-in code private.

3. What Hosirx is

Hosirx is a platform that connects customers with independent pharmacies and hospitals. The pharmacy or hospital, not Hosirx, supplies the medicine or care, sets its own prices and fees, and is responsible for it. Nothing on Hosirx is medical advice.

4. Ordering medicine

  • Some medicines need a valid prescription. Upload a clear photo of it. A pharmacist will check it.
  • A pharmacist may refuse or change an order for safety or legal reasons. If so, you will be told and refunded for anything not supplied.
  • Product pictures and descriptions are a guide. Read the label and leaflet before use.

5. Hospital visits

Registering or paying through Hosirx reserves a visit or registration with the hospital. The hospital decides who is seen and when, and may change a time. Video, voice and chat consultations are available in the Hosirx app where offered.

6. Payment

Prices and fees are shown in naira before you pay. Payments are handled by a payment processor. Hosirx does not store your full card details. Delivery fees, where charged, are shown at checkout.

7. Rules for everyone

  • Do not use Hosirx for anything unlawful, to buy medicine for resale, or to misuse prescription medicine.
  • Do not upload someone else's prescription or personal details without their permission.
  • Do not try to break, copy or overload the service.

8. Extra rules for partners

  • Pharmacies must hold a valid pharmacy registration and licence, and be supervised by a registered pharmacist.
  • Hospitals and clinics must hold valid facility registration, and their doctors must be properly licensed.

  • You are responsible for the products, care and delivery you provide, and for your own legal and tax duties.
  • We may review, pause or remove a listing that breaks these terms or puts people at risk.
